Dinner (5–9 PM) LARGE MEALS CRASH 26g:1u 150 Holding ratio HOLD 1:26 — the problem is bolus SHAPE, not the ratio. Crashes are entirely carb-load dependent: dinners ≥50 g went <80 in 4/4, crashed <70 in 3/4, median nadir 64, median 4 h end 203; dinners <50 g went <80 in 1/9, crashed 0/9, median nadir 92, median end 178. Target BG for this segment is 130 on the pump, higher than the 110 used elsewhere.
Night (9 PM+) LATE-EVE HIGHS 26g:1u 150 Sleep schedule proposed Values shown are the pump's 5 PM segment (1:26 / ISF 150), which governs 9 PM–midnight. AFTER MIDNIGHT the pump switches to the 12 AM segment: ISF 90, i.e. corrections become substantially stronger — a 2 AM correction using 150 will under-dose. Under-dosing is the safe direction, which is why 150 is published here, but check the clock before correcting.
