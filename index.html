Over Sep 7–20 there were ZERO nadirs <70 across 34 dinner boluses and zero occasions ending <80, while 13/17 ended >180 with median BG 174 -> 202 over 4 h. Night (9 PM+) LATE-EVE HIGHS 26g:1u 150 Sleep 9 PM restored Values shown are the pump's 5 PM segment (1:26 / ISF 150 / target 130), which governs 9 PM–midnight. AFTER MIDNIGHT the pump switches to the 12 AM segment: ISF 90 and target 110, i.e. corrections become substantially stronger — a 2 AM correction using 150/130 will under-dose. Under-dosing is the safe direction, which is why the evening values are published here, but check the clock before correcting. 31 of the 32 events this period were corrections, not meals. The Sleep schedule was moved back to 9:00 PM around Sep 5 and it WORKED: 8 PM–midnight basal went 0.47 -> 0.80 u/night and the carb-free evening rise fell from +45 to +32 (nights rising >60 mg/dL: 5/12 -> 2/12). KEEP the 9 PM start. The 9 PM–midnight block still reads 67% >180 only because dinner hands it off high — that is a dinner-ratio problem, not an evening one. Small hours are healthy and must be protected: 3–8 AM is 0% >180 at a median of 117, but 6 of 13 nights dipped below 80 (nadirs 67–81), so do NOT tighten anything that acts overnight.
